How to build a gym habit that lasts.

Practical steps for making movement a repeatable part of your week.

Start smaller than you think

A routine doesn't need to be intense to be useful. Begin with a realistic number of sessions and a duration that fits your schedule.

Three habits to try

  • Put workouts in your calendar
  • Prepare clothes and water beforehand
  • Record sessions and celebrate consistency

Adjust, don't quit

If a week gets busy, shorten a session or move it to another day. A flexible plan is easier to return to than an all-or-nothing approach.
