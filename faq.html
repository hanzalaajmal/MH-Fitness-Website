Questions? We've got you.

Helpful answers for your first visit and membership planning.

I'm new to the gym. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Start with the basics, use manageable weights, and ask for guidance whenever you're unsure about a movement.

What should I bring for my first workout?

Wear comfortable training clothes and suitable shoes. Bring water and a small towel, and arrive a little early to get familiar with the space.

How often should I work out?

It depends on your experience, goals, health, and schedule. Start at a manageable frequency and build gradually, allowing time for recovery.

Can I ask about personal coaching?

Yes. Use the contact page to describe your goals and availability so the team can discuss possible options.

Are the prices on this website final?

No. This is a student website concept, so the listed prices and schedule are sample content.

How do I become a member?

Visit the Join page and complete the enquiry form. It is a static demonstration and does not submit information to a server.
